Personas

Simulation profiles, calibrated to clinical reality

Personas drive the agent under test through configurable tone, speech rate, audio quality, and code-switching. Each profile is content-addressed — runs cite (persona_sha · agent_digest · suite_version) as a primary key.

Calibration axes

The four knobs every persona exposes

Locked in 2025-Q1 after a 12-run ablation; any further axis requires an ADR.

Tone
neutral · frustrated · anxious · cheerful · terse

Drives the LLM judge's prompt template. Anxious + frustrated are the highest-yield in catching brittle agents.

Speech rate
115–195 WPM (Gaussian per persona)

Below 130 WPM stresses VAD end-of-utterance heuristics; above 175 stresses ASR accuracy.

Call quality
clean · mobile · hearing-aid · degraded

Models jitter, packet loss, dropout, and per-band attenuation. Hearing-aid is the highest-value coverage gap for most teams.

Languages
en-US · es-MX · code-switched

Code-switched profiles surface ASR/LLM drift in clinical vocabulary (allergens, anticoagulants, dosing units).
